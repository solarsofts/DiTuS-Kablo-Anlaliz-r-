## 1. Referenced standards and technical publications

DiTuS Kablo Analizör contains engineering implementations, scope checks,
validation oracles, terminology, and traceability references associated with
technical publications including, as applicable to a given release:

- IEC 60287 family — electric cable current rating and associated losses and
  thermal resistance;
- IEC 60853 family — cyclic and emergency rating of cables;
- IEEE Std 575 and its revision lineage — bonding of shields and sheaths of
  single-conductor power cables; and
- CIGRE technical publications cited by the project documentation, including
  CIGRE TB 797 where identified by the calculation-scope documents.

The exact edition and clause identifiers used by a release are recorded in the
project's calculation-standard register and engineering-scope documentation.

## 2. No standards text is distributed

The project does not purport to distribute, reproduce, replace, or sublicense
the copyrighted standards themselves. Standard numbers, clause identifiers,
short technical labels, independently written engineering summaries, and
independently implemented equations or algorithms are used for engineering
traceability.

No statement in the project grants a right to reproduce or distribute a
third-party standard. Users must obtain lawful access to the authoritative
standard and the edition applicable to their work.

## 3. Authority of the standard

If project documentation, software behavior, an engineering summary, or a
third-party interpretation conflicts with the authoritative standard, the
standard and applicable law/regulation control. DiTuS result-authority labels
are software engineering controls; they are not certifications by a standards
organization.

IEC, IEEE, CIGRE, standard numbers, publication names, and other third-party

## 5. Licensed-source workflow

Where implementation requires values or information that cannot lawfully be
embedded in the open-source package, DiTuS may require user-supplied,
source-referenced data or may mark the calculation as reference-only,
incomplete, limited-authority, or blocked. The package does not grant users a
license to any external standard or manufacturer publication.
